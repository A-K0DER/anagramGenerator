# Backlink outreach plan (preparation only, nothing sent)

Status: **not started.** This document is the prep work. No emails have been sent and no sites contacted.

## Principles
- Ask for links only where the page is genuinely useful to that site's readers. One personal, relevant email beats a mass blast.
- No paid links, link exchanges at scale, comment spam or directory farms. They risk a manual penalty and rarely help.
- Link to the specific page that fits: `/anagram-solver/`, `/word-finder/` or `/anagram-checker/`, not just the homepage.

## Target types
Verify each site is active, accepts suggestions, and does not already list you before writing.

| Type | What to look for | Page to suggest |
|---|---|---|
| Word-game resource pages | "Helpful tools", "Scrabble resources" and "word game links" pages on game blogs and fan sites | `/word-finder/` |
| Vocabulary and ESL teaching sites | Lesson plans or "wordplay activities" pages for teachers | `/anagram-solver/`, `/anagram-checker/` |
| Puzzle and crossword blogs | Posts on cryptic clue-solving, "tools I use" lists | `/anagram-solver/` |
| Wikipedia-style or library guides | Research guides on wordplay, linguistics or recreational mathematics | `/anagram-solver/` |
| Roundups and "best anagram solver" articles | Existing comparison posts that omit you | `/compare/` |
| Educator communities | Teacher forums and newsletters (read each community's self-promotion rules first) | `/anagram-checker/` |

Find candidates with searches such as `"anagram" "useful links"`, `"word games" resources`, `cryptic crossword tips blog` and `"anagram solver" best tools`. Keep a sheet with: site, contact, page URL, date contacted, outcome.

## Linkable assets to build first
Outreach lands better when there is something worth citing. Candidates, in order of effort:
1. A "Famous anagrams" page with verified examples and a short note on each.
2. A printable cryptic-clue anagram cheat sheet (PDF) for teachers.
3. A short guide to Scrabble hooks and 7-letter word families, linking into the word finder.

## Email template (adapt every time)
Subject: Suggestion for your [page title] page

> Hi [name],
>
> I read your page on [specific topic] and liked [one specific detail].
>
> I run a free, browser-based tool, [Anagram Checker](https://theanagramgenerator.com/anagram-checker/), that tells you whether two phrases use the same letters. It needs no sign-up and nothing is uploaded. It might suit readers of that page, in case you ever update the list.
>
> No problem at all if it is not a fit. Thanks for the useful page.
>
> [name]

Follow up once after about two weeks, then stop.

## Tracking
Record outcomes in the sheet. After about 8 weeks, check Google Search Console under Links to see which links appeared, and which pages are getting impressions.
